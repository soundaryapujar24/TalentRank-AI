How the screening works

1. Resumes come from the AI CV builder (My Generated CVs) or an upload (My Uploaded Resumes). Text is parsed and personal details are hidden.
2. SBERT compares meaning against the job description.
3. Keyword match checks the must-have skills.
4. Final score = 60% SBERT + 40% keywords.

Ranking depends on skills and experience only.
